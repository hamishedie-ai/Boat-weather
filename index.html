Wind is the average of all four models, in knots. Gust is the highest any model predicts. Limits are set for a 4.1 m tinny: wind under 8 kn and waves under 0.4 m is comfortable, over 13 kn or 0.8 m means stay in. Wind against tide chops things up more than the numbers show.
